The OData road show continues with a stop at SAP Analytics conference, IBIS 2016 in Carlsbad, CA. The previous stop was the Progress DataDirect Partner Summit in Redwood City, CA where Michael Pizzo from the OData Technical Committee co-presented with me to talk about OData specification updates and adoption across V2 and V4.x. For this event, we talked about the future of SAP Business Objects Universes in the context of data connectivity.
